# Ground Truth: Cents of Truth

**Where your donation dollar actually goes.**

The third app in the Ground Truth series. Before you give, see the charity's own numbers — how many cents of every dollar it spends reach the programs, what its top officer takes home, and what it costs to raise a dollar — taken from the IRS Form 990 filings charities must make public.

## The showcase number

**American Red Cross: 91 cents of every dollar it spends goes to programs.** That figure comes straight from the Red Cross's own Form 990 (fiscal year ended June 30, 2025). They had to publish it by law. This app just makes sure somebody reads it.

## The data rules

- **10 charities**, every figure taken from the organization's actual IRS Form 990 e-file data, cross-referenced on ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer.
- Cents to programs = program services ÷ total functional expenses, exactly as reported in Part IX of the filing. Top officer pay and average worker pay are read — or computed — from the filing itself, and labeled which.
- **No estimates, no third-party ratings, no summaries.** Anything that could not be verified from a filing is left out, not filled in.

## How it works

Pick a charity and see its filing's own arithmetic: cents to programs, top officer pay, average worker pay, and the cost of raising one dollar. No ads, no accounts, no selling your data.
